The card editor is laid out like the owl designer now: a rail with Colour, Pattern and Styles on the left, and one pane at a time, so it barely scrolls. An app adds its own panes with `panes`. The style-code box and "Use this style" are gone, and so is `shareLink`. `CopyCardLink` copies a link to the card builder instead, and goes wherever the app wants it. A pattern set to cover the whole card on a solid colour showed no pattern at all, and it does now.

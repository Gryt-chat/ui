`ConversationItem` now draws the client's direct-message row: a larger avatar with an optional presence dot, the time and a pin beside the title, the last message under it, and an unread count. New optional props: `meta`, `note`, `unread`, `pinned`, `hidden`, `presence`. `title` and `subtitle` take any node. `PresenceDot` is exported on its own too.

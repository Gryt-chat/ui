New: `Thumb`, the tab on the right edge of a phone screen that opens a drawer with its links at the bottom. It sits in the lower half, where your thumb already is, and opens on a tap because a swipe from the edge is the phone's own back gesture. gryt.chat has used it since GRYT-1708. Hide it on wide screens with `tabClassName`.
